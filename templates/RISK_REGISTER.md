## Risk review rules

- Review risks at every stage handoff and before release.
- Record evidence and assumptions supporting ratings.
- Escalate high-impact security, privacy, data-loss, legal and production risks.
- Do not mark a risk closed without evidence that it is resolved or formally accepted.
